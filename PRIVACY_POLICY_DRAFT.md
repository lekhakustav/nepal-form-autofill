# Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: 2026-06-04

Nepal Form Autofill helps users extract and review information from their own documents so they can fill online forms faster.

## Information We Process

The app may process:

- uploaded document photos or PDFs,
- extracted names, dates, addresses, gender, document numbers, and similar form fields,
- selected form type,
- basic usage logs needed to monitor service reliability and quota.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

- extract form data,
- show the user a review screen,
- prepare printable form summaries,
- send reviewed fields to the local browser automation runner when the user requests portal autofill,
- monitor abuse, errors, and service usage.

## Portal Autofill

The local browser automation runner opens a supported browser profile on the user's own computer and fills reviewed form data into supported portal fields. It uses the reviewed data only for the portal autofill session.

Portal autofill does not bypass CAPTCHA, login, OTP, payment, or final submission.

## Uploaded Files

The current intended policy is that uploaded files are processed for extraction and not saved permanently by the app. If production storage is added later, this policy must be updated before launch.

## Third-Party Services

The app may use AI/OCR providers such as Google Gemini or Google Cloud Vision to extract text from documents. These providers may process uploaded content according to their own service terms.

## User Responsibility

Users must review all extracted data before submitting any official form. Nepal Form Autofill is an assistant, not a government authority.
