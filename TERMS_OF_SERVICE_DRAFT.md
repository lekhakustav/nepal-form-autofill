# Terms of Service Draft

Last updated: 2026-06-04

## Service

Nepal Form Autofill is a document extraction and form autofill assistant. It helps users review information from their own documents and fill supported online form fields faster.

## Not a Government Service

Nepal Form Autofill is not a government website, not an official government portal, and not a replacement for official application requirements.

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for:

- uploading only documents they are allowed to use,
- reviewing all extracted fields,
- completing CAPTCHA, login, OTP, payment, appointment, and final submission manually,
- confirming all official form data before submitting.

## No Bypass

The service must not be used to bypass CAPTCHA, login, OTP, identity verification, payments, or official portal restrictions.

## Accuracy

AI/OCR extraction can make mistakes. The user must review and correct all fields before use.

## Payment

Pricing may be per form, per month, or by operator plan. Refund rules should be published before paid launch.

## Limitation

Nepal Form Autofill does not guarantee that any government or institutional application will be accepted.
